No automatic statutory tax calculation has been enabled on this evidence alone. The pilot spans two calendar years, and company classification, prior losses, allowances, taxable-profit adjustments, indirect-tax treatment and payment timing are unknown. Enter explicitly identified tax planning costs where supported; keep `tax_reviewed` false until an accountant documents the applicable policy.
