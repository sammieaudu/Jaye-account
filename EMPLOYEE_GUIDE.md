# Employee local test guide

Choose Ada Okafor in the Test as selector. Open Spend, select the correct entity and press New expense. Enter vendor, amount, date, department/category and business purpose; attach a synthetic PDF/JPEG/PNG under 8 MB. Save draft, inspect the record and submit for approval. Requests do not need a receipt yet but cannot be paid directly. Bills need an invoice number.

The record shows status, evidence and approval history. Returned items retain their history; additional evidence can be attached and resubmitted. Text/amount editing and reimbursement-date forecasts remain pending. Employees see only records they requested. To exercise reviewer steps during local testing, change the top test identity; production will not provide this identity selector.

Never enter real personal data or upload untrusted real documents here. Nothing is reimbursed by this local app.
