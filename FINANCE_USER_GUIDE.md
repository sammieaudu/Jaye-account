# Finance local test guide

Default identity Amara Eze is Finance manager. Select one legal entity at a time. In Approvals inspect purpose, evidence and policy warnings. Department manager review comes first; higher-value/out-of-budget/related-party items require CFO. You cannot approve your own request.

Approved expenses/bills may be scheduled from Payments. Export CSV is marked synthetic and cannot execute a bank payment. A distinct Payment releaser confirms a synthetic bank reference. Import a debit statement CSV in Payments, then use the Reconciler identity to select an exact unmatched row. Finance may post only reconciled spend; both expense and settlement entries post together and are immutable.

General ledger links back to source spends. Reports contains trial balance, P&L and balance sheet. Spend and trial balance export as CSV. Budgets separates commitments from posted actuals. Audit trail verifies the stored hash chain. Full PO matching, accrual recognition before settlement, taxes, foreign currency and production reporting are pending. Do not use these reports as statutory books.
