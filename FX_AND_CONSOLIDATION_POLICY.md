# FX and consolidation · decision draft

Group presentation currency is intended to be USD; parent functional currency USD and Nigerian subsidiary NGN. Local spend validates entity currency and refuses foreign-currency inputs. No demo exchange rate is used, no displayed cash balances are summed across currencies, and consolidation is gated.

Accountants must approve rate sources, rate types, effective dates, rational/decimal rounding, override approval, settlement gains, revaluation/reversal, closing/average/historical translation, CTA and investment/intercompany eliminations. Constant-currency reference date/rate must be signed off. Freeze rates, source balances and eliminations per consolidation version; unmatched intercompany balances must block approval. These are design requirements, not tested implementations.
