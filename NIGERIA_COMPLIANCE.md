# Nigeria compliance · adviser work queue

The supplied brief identifies income tax/classification, Development Levy, VAT, WHT, PAYE, pension/employer contributions, transfer pricing, NOTAP, CAC, audited statements, e-invoicing and NDPA review. References to legislation, effective dates and future e-invoicing rollout in the brief are inputs requiring fresh authoritative verification; this app has not established applicability, rates or deadlines.

Nigerian accountant/payroll adviser/counsel must approve versioned rule packs and relevant evidence requirements. No liability calculation, automatic filing, remittance, compliance calendar or government portal interaction is implemented. Government spend is deliberately gated until legal basis, official receipt and independent CFO review controls exist.
