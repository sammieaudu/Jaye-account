# Group structure

GoJaye Inc. is the proposed Delaware USD parent, fundraising entity and IP owner. GoJaye Nigeria Ltd is the proposed wholly owned NGN operating subsidiary. Ash Capital & Technology Ltd is excluded unless founders and counsel confirm a legal rename/transition. Entity registration and agreements are not verified by this app.

Local seed IDs are `inc` and `ng`; all financial tables and queries are entity-scoped. The consolidated selector is a gated reporting destination. It never combines nominal USD and NGN or converts using an invented rate. Future entities require schema/configuration generalization beyond the two-entity local validation enum.

The opening capital fixtures are synthetic balanced test entries, not evidence of fundraising or issued shares. Equity instruments, capital importation, loans, CCI and IP charges are not enabled.
